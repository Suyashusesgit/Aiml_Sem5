## 2. Why Are There Two Paper Versions / Reports?

### Reason 1: Double-Blind Peer Review (Anonymized vs. Camera-Ready)
Most IEEE Transactions journals (e.g., *IEEE TIP*, *IEEE TCSVT*, *IEEE TPAMI*, *IEEE TMM*) enforce strict **double-blind peer review**:
* Reviewers must **not** know the identity or institution of the authors to prevent prestige or geographic bias.
* **`IEEE_Paper_To_Submit_Anonymized.pdf`**: Strips author names, affiliations, and acknowledgments. **This is the one you submit today.**
* **`IEEE_Paper_CameraReady_With_Authors.pdf`**: Restores all author names, institutional addresses, and supervisor acknowledgments for the final published volume.

### Reason 2: Student Academic Completion Reports vs. Journal Manuscript
In your `report/` workspace directory, you may notice separate reports:
* **`Suyash_Srivastav_Completion_Report.pdf`** & **`Ananya_Kasaudhan_Completion_Report.pdf`**: These are your individual 5th-semester Capstone project completion reports for college submission and internal grading at Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya.
* **`Project_Triathlon_Comprehensive_Deliverables_Report.pdf`**: The master technical report covering the entire engineering milestone triathlon.
* **`IEEE_Paper_*.pdf`**: The formal 17-page academic research manuscript targeted for IEEE journal publication.
