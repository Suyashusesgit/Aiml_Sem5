## Audit Conclusions

1. **Phase 0 Baseline Claims (C1–C5) are Fully Validated**: The original team report metrics accurately reflect the true behavior of the low-light algorithm selection problem.
2. **"Tuned RF 0.430 dB" (C6) was Not Reproduced Under Scene-Grouped CV**: Under scene-grouped CV, folds asserted disjoint, RF is not demonstrably better in-distribution than Logistic Regression (95% bootstrap CI $[-0.272, +0.064]$ dB spans zero).
3. **The Champion is Multinomial Logistic Regression**: Verified empirically across 10 random seeds, 4 grouping thresholds, and external zero-shot transfer ($d_{\text{real\_test}} = 0.0000$ dB with 100% agreement on non-duplicate pairs; reverse transfer $d_{\text{reverse}} = -0.5669$ dB before exclusion and $+0.1380$ dB after exclusion). RF not demonstrably better in-distribution.
